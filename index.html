      Neural Networks

      Neural networks are computing systems inspired by biological neural networks. They form the foundation of deep learning and modern AI.

      What are Neural Networks?

      Neural networks consist of layers of interconnected nodes (neurons) that process information using weighted connections and activation functions.

      The Perceptron

      The basic building block of neural networks, invented by Frank Rosenblatt in 1957.

      output = activation(sum(weights * inputs) + bias)

      Backpropagation

      The algorithm used to train neural networks by propagating errors backward through the network.

      Deep Learning

      Deep learning uses multi-layered neural networks to solve complex problems in vision, language, and decision making.
